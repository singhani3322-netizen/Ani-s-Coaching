Coaching Principles
Show
The 10% Rule
Never increase weekly running distance by more than 10% versus last week. Prevents the leading cause of running injuries.
The 80/20 Rule
Elite runners keep 80% of training at easy pace, only 20% moderate or hard. Most amateurs get this backwards.
Interference Effect
Heavy leg day plus hard run within 48 hours degrades both. Space leg day and hard runs 2 days apart.
Combined Load
Your body doesn't distinguish gym stress from running stress. Total weekly load matters more than individual sessions.
Recovery Priority
Adaptation happens during rest, not during training. Rest days are when you actually get stronger.
Smart Compensation
If you underperform a session, don't just skip it — spread the difference across upcoming sessions to stay on track without overloading.
