🎓 About the Coaching Logic (tap)
📏 The 10% Rule
Never increase weekly running distance by more than 10% vs. last week. Prevents #1 cause of running injuries (doing too much too soon).
⚖️ The 80/20 Rule
Elite endurance athletes run ~80% at easy pace, only 20% moderate/hard. Most amateurs get this backwards, which hurts progress and increases injury risk.
⚠️ The Interference Effect
Heavy leg day + hard run within 24-48 hours = both suffer. Fatigued muscles can't perform either well and injury risk skyrockets. Schedule them apart!
😴 Why Recovery Days Matter
Muscles grow and adapt during rest, not during training. Elite athletes prioritize recovery. Aim for at least 1 full rest day per week.
📊 Combined Training Load
Your body doesn't distinguish "running stress" from "gym stress"—it accumulates. Monitoring combined load prevents overtraining better than tracking each separately.
