The 10% Rule

80% easy, 20% hard. The pattern elite runners actually use.
Interference Effect
Heavy leg day + hard run within 48hrs hurts both. Kept apart.
Grace Over Guilt
Missed sessions get redistributed, not judged.
Baseline-Driven
Your pace zones scale from YOUR easy run, not generic paces.
Baseline Evolution
